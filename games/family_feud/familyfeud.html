🎮 Two Player Game Instructions

  1. Enter Player Names
    Player 1 and Player 2 will be asked to enter their family names before the game begins.
  2. Game Layout
    Player 1 will appear on the left side, Player 2 on the right. The question appears in the center.
  3. Buzz In!
    When the question is displayed, press your key to buzz in:
    • 🅳 – Player 1
    • 🅻 – Player 2
    The first to press answers first.
  4. Answering
    Correct answer = point! Wrong answer = the other player gets a chance.
  5. Game Objective
    Compete to get the most points. The player with the highest score wins!
